## E3 · Data discipline

```
A counter-intuitive design choice:

When the answer isn't in the knowledge base, our AI says so outright —
"this system will not fabricate using general knowledge or training data."

Every rule check returns one of three states: met / unmet / data missing.
"Data missing" is not "not qualified" — the system tells you which document to supply.
Every verdict keeps the original policy wording, so it stays traceable.

In a business where inventing a threshold can cost a client real money,
refusing to invent is a feature.

#ZhongceTong
```
